Frequently asked questions

Is DestroCorp a company or a collection of apps?

DestroCorp is the parent studio and single legal entity. Kirvyn, AuditScan and Buildopsy are its brands — each with its own domain and roadmap, all sharing one privacy and accountability standard.

Which product should I start with?

Join or host a community → Kirvyn. Need to review code or gate AI PRs → AuditScan. Want to learn systems design → Buildopsy.

How do payments work?

All payments are processed by our payment processor. We never store card or UPI credentials. Digital access is granted instantly; eligible refunds are processed to the original source within 5–7 business days.

How do I exercise my privacy rights?

Email grievance@destrocorp.com or use the data-rights form. We honour access, correction, erasure and consent-withdrawal requests under India's DPDP Act, the EU/UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, LGPD, PIPEDA and Australia's Privacy Act.

Do you sell data or run ads?

No. We do not sell personal data, run third-party advertising, or use cross-site trackers on this site. Analytics, if ever enabled, will be first-party and opt-in only.
