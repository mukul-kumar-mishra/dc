Cookie Policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026

Default: no optional cookies. This site stores only your consent choice (first-party, on your device). Analytics load only if you opt in. There are no advertising or cross-site tracking cookies — and no third-party scripts on this page at all.

1. What we store

CategoryWhatDefault
NecessaryYour consent choice (destrocorp-consent-v1, localStorage)Always on
PreferencesReserved for future UI preferences (theme, locale)Off
AnalyticsReserved for future first-party, cookieless-optional metricsOff (opt-in)
MarketingNot used. Listed only for transparency.Off, permanently

2. Signals we honour

Do-Not-Track (DNT) and Global Privacy Control (GPC) are respected: when detected, optional categories stay off and the banner tells you so.

3. Change your choice

Click Cookie settings in the footer anytime, or clear your browser's site data to reset to “undecided”. Withdrawing consent is as easy as giving it.

4. Product apps and contact form

Kirvyn, AuditScan and Buildopsy apps use strictly-necessary session/authentication storage to keep you signed in and prevent fraud. Their own cookie details are documented on each domain; the same opt-in principle applies.

Our contact form is hosted by Google Forms — opening it loads Google's pages, and entries you submit are stored by Google under their terms. We link to it instead of embedding it, so Google loads nothing on our pages until you choose to click.
