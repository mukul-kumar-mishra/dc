About DestroCorp

DestroCorp · India · support@destrocorp.com

DestroCorp runs two products and one publication: Kirvyn (kirvyn.com) — paid community clubs with chat, events and UPI payments; AuditScan (auditscan.sh) — code audits where findings cite file and line numbers; and Buildopsy (buildopsy.com) — a blog with systems-design essays and short paid courses.

One studio handles billing, terms and support for everything listed here. Each product and the publication keep their own site — see Kirvyn, AuditScan and Buildopsy. Policies that apply everywhere are linked below: Privacy, Terms, Refunds, Shipping, Cookies.
