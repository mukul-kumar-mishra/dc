About DestroCorp

Independent software studio · India · support@destrocorp.com

DESTROCORP is a remote-first independent software studio based in India operating three brands: Kirvyn (kirvyn.com) — a micro-club community platform; AuditScan (auditscan.sh) — a deterministic code-verification engine; and Buildopsy (buildopsy.com) — a systems-design publication with paid courses.

How we're organised

One legal entity owns all three brands, so customers, regulators and payment partners deal with a single accountable operator. Each brand keeps its own domain, roadmap and team rituals. Shared across all: one privacy standard (DPDP Act 2023 + GDPR-aligned), one payments standard (secure processing, no card storage), one grievance standard (24-hour acknowledgement, 15-day resolution).

What we believe

  • Verify, don't trust. Every AuditScan finding links to file:line; every Buildopsy claim links to code; every Kirvyn payment links to a payment receipt.
  • Small is a feature. 16-seat clubs, focused scans, short courses — depth over scale.
  • Privacy is the product. No ads, no data selling, no dark patterns. Consent is opt-in and revocable.
