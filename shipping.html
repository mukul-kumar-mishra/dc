Shipping Policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026

No physical shipping. DestroCorp sells only digital services. Nothing is couriered, posted or handed over in person.

How delivery works

  • Kirvyn: club seats and chat access open in your account instantly after our payment processor confirms payment; a receipt goes to your registered email.
  • AuditScan: scan quotas and reports appear in your dashboard instantly after payment.
  • Buildopsy: course enrolments open in your account instantly after payment.

Delivery issues

If access doesn't appear within 15 minutes of a successful payment, email support@destrocorp.com with your payment ID — we resolve within 24 hours, and eligible cases are refunded per the Refund Policy.
