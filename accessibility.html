Accessibility statement

Last updated: 24 September 2026

DestroCorp targets WCAG 2.2 AA across destrocorp.com and, progressively, all brand domains. This site uses semantic HTML, a skip link, keyboard-operable controls, visible focus, sufficient colour contrast, and honours prefers-reduced-motion. Found a barrier? Email support@destrocorp.com with the page URL and your assistive technology — we aim to fix critical barriers within 15 days.
