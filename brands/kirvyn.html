Why hosts pick micro-clubs

  • Capped at 16 seats, priced by you — small groups stay conversational and every seat is paid.
    Seats are allocated on payment confirmation; you set house rules.
  • Chat + events + live rooms in one place — no juggling three apps.

Frequently asked questions

How do I join a club?

Browse clubs on kirvyn.com, pick one, pay via UPI — your seat is confirmed instantly and chat access opens immediately.

How do refunds work for club seats?

Duplicate payments or seats never provisioned are refunded within 7 days of request to support@destrocorp.com. See the Refund Policy.

How is my chat data handled?

Chat messages are retained 90 days then auto-deleted. You can request earlier erasure via grievance@destrocorp.com.
